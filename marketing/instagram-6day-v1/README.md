# QualiFi Instagram — 6-day TR/EN creative pack

Prepared on 14 November 2026. Two separate language sets, each with three
five-slide carousels, three 18/20/18-second Reels, and six Story backgrounds.
Native editable PowerPoint templates, ready PNGs/MP4s, the brand guide, and an
empty measurement workbook were delivered as downloadable artifacts in chat.

This folder versions the brand rules, copy, scheduling and measurement plan.
It does not change website functionality, publish Instagram posts, run ads,
or write to Canva. Canva was reported installed, but its design tools were
not callable in this chat; Canva account import remains unverified.

- [Brand system](brand-identity.md)
- [Six-day playbook](six-day-playbook.md)
- [All TR/EN copy and scene text](content.json)
- [Posting manifest and links](posting-plan.csv)

Content describes the product as early-access development; no live agent or
integration, collection guarantee, fabricated testimonial or reach claim.
Generated videos contain silent audio, with separate voiceover scripts.
Native Instagram poll/question/link stickers must be added when posting.

Independent read-only copy/LIFT and measurement reviews informed the final
waiting-list CTAs, audience clarity, bio-vs-Story attribution, and matched
consented-visitor funnel calculations. These are source/image checks, not a
real human five-second test. No real Instagram or marketing results exist yet.

The 12 November website audit remains separate: some CTA events were rejected,
uploads were unavailable, calendar was unconfigured and visitor confirmation
email absent. This creative pack does not claim to fix those issues.
